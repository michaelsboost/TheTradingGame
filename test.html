Advanced details

Know before you trade

Real trading involves real money. Use this tool to explore risk, expectancy, losing streaks and possible account outcomes—not as a signal to trade.

What this tool can teach you

Explore how win rate, reward-to-risk, position sizing, trading costs and random sequences of wins and losses affect your account over time.

What you still need to research
  • Market structure, price action, chart patterns and indicators
  • Volume, liquidity, momentum and volatility
  • Economic releases, news and market conditions
  • Entry and exit rules, backtesting and forward testing
  • Trading psychology, discipline, order execution and broker requirements

Use evidence from your own testing to estimate win rate and costs. Practice in a demo account before considering live trading.

Positive simulated expectancy does not guarantee profitability. Real outcomes can differ because of changing markets, gaps, slippage, fees and execution. Leveraged futures can result in losses beyond your deposit. Never risk money you cannot afford to lose.
